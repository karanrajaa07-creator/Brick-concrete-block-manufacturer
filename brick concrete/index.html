Engineering Workflow

From Raw Quarry Aggregates to Cured Blocks

Our 5-stage automated production line combines high-tonnage vibro-compaction with sealed steam curing for rock-solid durability.

01

RAW SIFTING & MIXING

Aggregates, high-grade Portland cement, and additives undergo laser grain sifting and moisture control.

02

HIGH-PRESSURE PRESS

Hydraulic presses deliver 180 tons of pressure combined with multi-axis vibration to eliminate all air voids.

03

STEAM CURING CELLS

Freshly molded blocks enter controlled autoclave chambers where high humidity cures them to 80% strength in 24h.

04

UTM LAB TESTING

Random samples from every batch undergo destructive load crushes and water absorption verification.

05

STRAPPING & DISPATCH

Automated palletizers wrap, strap, and load units onto our crane-equipped trucks for direct site delivery.
