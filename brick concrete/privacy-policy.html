Commercial Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Version 4.2

1. Scope of Privacy Policy

Titan Brick & Concrete Industries Inc. ("Titan", "we", "our", "us") values the confidentiality and intellectual property of commercial general contractors, civil engineers, real estate developers, and municipal agencies that engage with our website, request quotations, or open wholesale trade accounts.

2. Information We Collect

We collect business and technical procurement details necessary to fulfill commercial manufacturing quotes, lab test scheduling, and flatbed deliveries:

  • Corporate legal business name, contractor license numbers, and tax identification IDs.
  • Site superintendent, purchasing officer, and project manager contact details (telephone, email).
  • Jobsite geographical coordinates, delivery staging addresses, and unloading access constraints.
  • Architectural masonry schedules, structural CAD specifications, and target compressive benchmarks.

3. Commercial Use of Data

Information supplied via our quote calculators, contact forms, or contractor portal is strictly utilized for structural load compatibility analysis, production line queue assignment, freight rate computation, and electronic Net-30 credit underwriting. We do not sell, rent, or trade contractor project schedules or client contact data to third-party marketing brokers.

4. Data Security Protocols

All quotation submissions, portal communications, and electronic invoices are transmitted via 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) encryption and stored in SOC 2 Type II compliant industrial cloud architectures.

5. Inquiries & Contact

For legal inquiries regarding commercial data handling, please contact our corporate compliance counsel at privacy@titanbrickconcrete.com or write to Titan Brick & Concrete Industries Inc., 4200 Industrial Parkway, Sector 9 Manufacturing Corridor.
