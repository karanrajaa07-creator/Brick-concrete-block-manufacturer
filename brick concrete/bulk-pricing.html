Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)

As a commercial manufacturer, our automated press and kiln lines produce units in bulk palletized batches:

  • Solid / Hollow Concrete Blocks: 5 Pallets (~300 - 375 Units)
  • AAC Lightweight Blocks: 4 Pallets (~216 Units)
  • Clay Kiln & Fly Ash Bricks: 6 Pallets (~3,000 Bricks)
  • Interlocking Heavy Pavers: 50 sq. meters (~1,800 Pavers)
  • Custom CNC Mold Precast: Subject to mold tooling MOQ

Freight & Transportation Charges

  • Zone 3 (60 - 120 Miles): Flat $320 / Flatbed Trip
