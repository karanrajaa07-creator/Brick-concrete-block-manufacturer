Production Excellence

Precision Manufacturing & Quality Control

Every pallet produced at Titan Brick is formulated with calibrated aggregate gradients, hydraulic vibro-pressed, steam cured, and verified with destructive laboratory crushing tests.

Phase 01

Automated Batching

Computer-controlled weigh-hoppers blend crushed quartzite, river sand, and OPC 53 cement to ± 0.5% precision.

Phase 02

180-Ton Hydraulic Pressing

German vibro-press machinery exerts 180 tonnes of pressure to eliminate structural air voids and ensure dense cores.

Phase 03

Autoclave Steam Curing

Fresh green blocks enter pressurized humidity cells to achieve 28-day hydraulic strength within 24 hours.

Phase 04

Destructive UTM Testing

Calibrated 3,000 kN compression load cell validates batch PSI ratings and issues ASTM C90 certified compliance sheets.
