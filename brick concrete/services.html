Full Industrial Manufacturing Capabilities

Manufacturing & Engineering Services

From custom architectural mold fabrication and destructive compressive lab testing to high-tonnage bulk supply and just-in-time site dispatch.

Service 01

Automated Brick Manufacturing

We operate automated continuous-fire tunnel kilns that maintain precise 1,050°C temperatures with computer-controlled oxygenation. This process eliminates core black-coring and produces vitrified clay bricks that resist frost, thermal cycling, and chemical efflorescence.

Capacity: 45 Million wire-cut and machine-pressed clay bricks annually
Compressive ratings up to Class 200 (20 N/mm²) for load-bearing facades
Automated robotic setting and pallet strapping with moisture barrier wraps
Service 02

Concrete Block Manufacturing

High-throughput hydraulic vibro-press machinery exerting 180 tons of compaction force. We cast standard 4", 6", 8" solid and hollow units, as well as lightweight micro-cellular AAC blocks with zero structural voids.

Strict ± 1mm dimensional tolerance ensuring plumb masonry courses
Sealed autoclave steam curing accelerating 28-day hydration within 24 hours
Certified to ASTM C90 and ASTM C129 load-bearing specifications
Service 03

Custom Block Production & CNC Molds

When standard modular units don't meet complex architectural curvatures or infrastructure load points, our CNC tooling center designs custom hardened-steel molds. We manufacture bespoke precast shapes, interlocking radius retaining blocks, and split-face facade panels.

In-house CAD/CAM mold engineering with 7-day turnaround from blueprint
Ultra-high-strength mixes formulated up to M-60 Grade (60 MPa / 8,700 PSI)
Architectural pigments, exposed aggregate, and basalt fiber reinforcement
Service 04

Bulk Material Supply Solutions

For multi-phase residential communities, shopping centers, and commercial skyscrapers requiring hundreds of thousands of blocks delivered across months without stock shortages or site congestion.

Guaranteed fixed price lock-in for contracts up to 18 months
Dedicated yard reserving your exact batch pallets until site calls
Direct freight dispatch with knuckle-boom cranes for multi-floor placement
